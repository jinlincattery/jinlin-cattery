About us

A cattery built on care, transparency and healthy kittens

JinLin is a responsible cattery specialising in British Shorthair and Maine Coon cats. We raise every kitten in our family home, never in cages, and deliver them door-to-door to families across the UAE.

Our story

What began as a deep love for cats grew into a commitment to raise healthy, well-tempered kittens with complete transparency.

♡

Raised at home, never caged

Our kittens grow up underfoot in a calm family home, handled daily and socialised from birth.

§

Carefully selected

We select our cats for type, coat quality and stable, affectionate temperament.

✓

Health-first breeding

Every kitten is vet-checked, vaccinated and microchipped before leaving, with full health records provided.

✈

International delivery, handled

We manage veterinary certificates, import permits and customs clearance, delivering safely across the UAE.

Our values

Three principles guide every kitten we place.

01

Transparency

Full documentation and honest communication at every stage.

02

Responsibility

We breed responsibly, in small numbers, and stand behind every kitten for life.

03

Care

Every kitten is family first — raised with love, patience and daily attention.
