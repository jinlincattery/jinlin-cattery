Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about buying a kitten from JinLin — health, delivery, reservation, pricing and aftercare.

Health & breeding

Are your kittens health-tested and vaccinated?
Yes. Every kitten is vet-checked, vaccinated, microchipped and comes with complete health documentation. Full health records are shared before handover.
At what age do kittens go to their new homes?
Kittens are typically ready from 12–16 weeks, once fully vaccinated, health-checked and prepared for travel.
How are your kittens raised?
Every kitten is raised at home, never in cages, with daily handling and socialisation from birth.

Delivery & import

How do you deliver kittens to the UAE?
We handle the complete international delivery process, including veterinary certificates, import permits and customs clearance, so your kitten arrives safely at your home in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or elsewhere in the UAE.
Is it safe to import a cat to the UAE?
Yes. We follow all UAE import requirements, including vaccinations, veterinary certificates and import permits, and manage customs clearance on arrival.
How long does delivery take?
Once your kitten is ready (fully vaccinated and health-checked), the international delivery and clearance process is typically completed within a planned timeframe. We keep you updated at every stage.

Reservation & pricing

How do I reserve a kitten?
Message us on WhatsApp with the breed and colour you're interested in. A reservation secures your kitten while we complete the health checks and travel paperwork.
How much does a British Shorthair or Maine Coon kitten cost?
Prices vary by breed, colour and lineage and are shared privately on enquiry. Contact us on WhatsApp for current availability and pricing.
Why are prices shared privately?
Each kitten's price reflects its unique colour, lineage and temperament. We share pricing privately so we can match you with the right kitten for your needs and budget.

Aftercare

Do you offer support after adoption?
Yes — we offer lifetime support and guidance on nutrition, settling in and care.
What documents do I receive with my kitten?
You receive a complete records pack, including health records, vaccination certificate and microchip details.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll respond promptly.
