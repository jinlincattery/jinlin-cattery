How it works

From enquiry to your home — a clear, five-step journey

Buying a kitten internationally can feel complex. We handle the entire journey — health checks, veterinary certificates, import permits and customs clearance — so you can simply welcome your companion home.

The five steps

01

Enquiry

Message us on WhatsApp with the breed, colour and timing you have in mind.

02

Virtual viewing

We arrange a video viewing of your kitten and their parents, sharing health records.

03

Reservation

A reservation secures your kitten while we complete health checks and travel paperwork.

04

Health & paperwork

Vaccinations, veterinary certificates, import permits and customs clearance are all completed for you.

05

Welcome home

Your kitten arrives at your home in the UAE with a complete records pack.
