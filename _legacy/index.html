Questions

Frequently asked

Are your kittens health-tested and vaccinated?
Yes. Every kitten is vet-checked, vaccinated, microchipped and comes with complete health documentation. Full health records are shared before handover.
How do you deliver kittens to the UAE?
We handle the complete international delivery process, including veterinary certificates, import permits and customs clearance, so your kitten arrives safely at your home in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or elsewhere in the UAE.
At what age do kittens go to their new homes?
Kittens are typically ready from 12–16 weeks, once fully vaccinated, health-checked and prepared for travel.
How do I reserve a kitten?
Message us on WhatsApp with the breed and colour you're interested in. A reservation secures your kitten while we complete the health checks and travel paperwork.
Do you offer support after adoption?
Yes — we offer lifetime support and guidance on nutrition, settling in and care.
